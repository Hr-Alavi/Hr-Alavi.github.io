VA4BHC (Virtual Assistant for Better Healthcare) is a national research project funded by the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation through the Agencia Estatal de Investigación, led by Top Health Tech (BEHEALTHCAREIT, S.L.).

Hamidreza contributed as a core team member, alongside his role as project manager for digital construction and health infrastructure at Top Health Tech (2022–2023).
